1: Introduction
The Workshop Energy Cost Estimator is a Python-based application developed to help workshop operators and students estimate the energy consumption and electricity cost of machines and equipment used in technical workshops.

The system allows users to input the power rating and daily operating hours of each machine, calculates daily and monthly energy usage in kilowatt-hours (kWh), and estimates the corresponding electricity cost based on the local tariff.

2:Problem Statement
Many technical workshops operate without accurate knowledge of how much electricity their machines consume. This often leads to high and unexpected electricity bills, inefficient use of equipment, and difficulty identifying which machines contribute most to energy costs.

Manual estimation is time-consuming and prone to errors. This project provides a simple digital solution that allows users to quickly calculate energy consumption and cost, identify high-energy machines, and receive practical energy-saving suggestions.

3: Aim and Objectives
The aim of this project is to develop a simple Python application that estimates the energy consumption and electricity cost of workshop machines.

The specific objectives are to:
- Accept machine name, power rating (watts), and daily operating hours from the user
- Calculate daily and monthly energy consumption in kWh
- Calculate the monthly electricity cost based on a user-defined tariff
- Display results in a clear tabular format
- Identify the machine with the highest energy consumption
- Suggest potential energy and cost savings if the highest-consuming machine runs one hour less per day
- Rate the overall workshop energy usage as Low, Moderate, or High

4:System Features
- Multi-machine data entry
- Daily and monthly energy consumption calculation
- Electricity cost estimation based on local tariff
- Clear tabular display of results
- Identification of the biggest energy-consuming machine
- Energy-saving recommendation (1-hour reduction scenario)
- Workshop energy usage rating (Low / Moderate / High)
- Input validation to prevent program crashes
